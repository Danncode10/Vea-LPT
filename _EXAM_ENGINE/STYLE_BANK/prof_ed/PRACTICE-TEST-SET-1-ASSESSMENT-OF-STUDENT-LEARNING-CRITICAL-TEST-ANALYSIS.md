GUIDELINES FOR WRITING COMPLETION TEST

1. Ensure that completion items have only one possible correct
   answer.
2. Ensure that the fill-in is plausible to the knowledgeable student;
   it should not be based on trick data or trivia.
3. Use completion that are specific terms (concept, person, place,
   object) since an item requiring a more general phrase may elicit
   more subjective responses and be harder to score.
4. Use one blank, or certainly no more than two, in any item, since
   more than two blanks leads to confusion and ambiguity.
5. Avoid open-ended item. There should be only one acceptable
   answer
6. The blank should be at the end or near the end of the sentence.

GUIDELINES IN CONSTRUCTING MATCHING
TYPE OF TEST

1. Match homogeneous not heterogeneous
2. The premises or stem (longer in construction than than the
   responses or options), responses or options must be in the second
   column (usually shorter).
3. The responses or options must be more than the stems to prevent
   the student from arriving at the answer by mere process of
   elimination.
4. Arrange the responses or option alphabetically or chronologically.
5. The direction of the test must be given. The examinees must know
   exactly what to do.
