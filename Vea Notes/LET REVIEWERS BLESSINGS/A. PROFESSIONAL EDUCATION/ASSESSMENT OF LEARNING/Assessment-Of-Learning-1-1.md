WHAT IS VALIDITY?
It is the ability of the test to measure what it purports to measure. There is no such thing that is a valid test by itself. It only becomes valid if it can measure the objectives it mean to measure.

--- Slide 10 ---
2.CRITERION VALIDITY
THE CRITERION VALIDITY OF THE TEST IS DETERMINED BY CORRELATING THE TEST TO ANOTHER TEST WHICH IS ALREADY KNOWN TO BE VALID AND RELIABLE.
This is determined statistically. The statistical tool commonly used is Pearson r. The r must be 0.75 for the test to be considered to have criterion validity.
