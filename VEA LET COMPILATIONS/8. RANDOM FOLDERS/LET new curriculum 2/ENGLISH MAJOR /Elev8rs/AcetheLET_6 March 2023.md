                             Meaning of ESP

•   Mackay and Mountford - English for a clear utilitarian purpose
•   Hutchinson and Waters - approach to language teaching in which all
    decisions as to content and method are based on the learner ’ s
    reason for learning.
•   Belcher - problems are unique to specific learners in specific contexts
•   Strevens ’ original definition of ESP - identifying its absolute and
    variable characteristics
•   Dudley-Evans and St John




                                                               elev8rstraining@gmail.com
Dudley Evans: Absolute Characteristics:

• Meets specific needs of learners.

• Makes use of underlying methodology and activities of the
  discipline it serves.

• Is centered on the language appropriate to these activities in
  terms of grammar, lexis, syntax, study skills, discourse, and
  genre.


                                                     elev8rstraining@gmail.com
Dudley Evans: Variable Characteristics:

• May be related to or designed for specific disciplines.
• May use, in particular situations, different methodologies from
  general English.
• Is likely to be designed for adult learners, either at an institute
  or at a workplace.
• Is generally designed for intermediate and advanced students.
• Most of the ESP courses assume some basic knowledge of the
  language systems.
