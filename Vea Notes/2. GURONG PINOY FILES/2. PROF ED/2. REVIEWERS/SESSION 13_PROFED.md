1. A group that has clear roles and mutual respect is likely in which stage?
	•	Storming
	•	Norming
	•	Forming
	•	Adjourning

TUCKMAN'S STAGES OF GROUP DEVELOPMENT (FSNPA)



















Stage
Key Characteristics / Description
Forming
Members are still adjusting to each other and the team.
Storming
Conflicts, disagreements, and misunderstandings happen.
Norming
Roles become clear; cooperation and mutual respect develop.
Performing
The group becomes highly productive and works well together.
Adjourning
The group completes its tasks and disbands.
