Approaches to classroom management
1. ASSERTIVE APPROACH - teacher specifies rules, clearly identifies
   expectations, uses firm voice, eye contact, gestures and touch and
   supplements them with verbal messages
2. BUSINESS ACADEMIC APPROACH - emphasizes the organization and
   management of students as they engage in academic work. Three major
   categories. establishes and communicates assignment, uses standards
   and procedures, monitors student work, gives feedback to students
3. BEHAVIOR MODIFICATION APPROACH - behavior is strengthened by
   systematic reinforcement
4. GROUP MANAGERIAL APPROACH - responds immediately to group
   student behavior (RIPPLE EFFECT)
5. ACCEPTANCE APPROACH - maintains that every individual needs to feel
   acceptance and belongingness
6. SUCCESS APPROACH – deals with psychological and social conditions
   instead of dealing with appropriate behavior and the consequences of
   such behavior. Teacher’s job is to help students make good choices.
