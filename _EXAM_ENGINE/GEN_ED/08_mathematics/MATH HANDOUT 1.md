      G. Word Problems

          Steps in solving word problems
       First: Comprehend the problem. Never solve a problem you did not understand. If you
have a clear understanding of the problem then you have solved 50% of it already. Sometimes
a drawing would help.
       Second: Identify the given with the correct label.
       Third: Identify the correct formula or operation to be used. Make the necessary
conversion of units at this point.
       Fourth: Derive the correct form of the formula for the specific requirement
       Fifth: Plug in all values into the formula and do the calculations.
