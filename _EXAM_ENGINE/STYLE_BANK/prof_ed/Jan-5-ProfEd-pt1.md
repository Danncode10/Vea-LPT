4.3 SHA shall be given to teachers and school heads/administrators assigned to a
hardship post provided that the total SHA shall not exceed twenty five percent
(25%) of the annual basic salary’. The allowable SHA shall be computed as
follows:

SHA = (Basic salary x rate per distance) x number of days present in a month over
number of working days in a month; wherein rate per distance is shown
hereunder:

Distance*     Rate per distance (% of basic salary)
5-10 km            15%
11-20 km           20%
21 km & above      25%
