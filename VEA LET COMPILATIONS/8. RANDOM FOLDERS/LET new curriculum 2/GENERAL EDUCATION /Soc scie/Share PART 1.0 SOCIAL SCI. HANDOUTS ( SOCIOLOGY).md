RELATIONSHIP AND BONDS IN SOCIETY
Gemeinschaft- Rural relationship
Relationship is personal where friendship and kinship is integrated in the relationship

Gesellschaft- Urban relationship
Relationship is impersonal, specialized and business-like.

Socialization
The acquisition of the norms and roles accepted of people in a particular society
