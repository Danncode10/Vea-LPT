How does an occlusion form?
 a. Cold air moving up from the ground
 b. Cold front pushing warm air up off the
 ground c. Unbalance electrical reactions in the
 air d. Cold and warm air mixing in the
 atmosphere
An Occluded Front forms when a warm air mass gets caught between two cold
air masses. The warm air mass rises as the cool air masses push and meet in
the middle. The temperature drops as the warm air mass is occluded, or “cut
off,” from the ground and pushed upward.
