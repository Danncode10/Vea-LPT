PROBABILITY RULES
There are four (4) basic probability rules as mentioned by Bluman (2019).
1. The probability of any event E is a number, either fraction or decimal, between
Oand 1.
2. If an event E cannot occur, then the probability is 0.
3. If an event E is certain, then the probability of E is 1.
4. The sum of the probabilities of all the outcomes in the sample space is 1.

B. The Addition Rule for Probability

Two events are mutually exclusive events if they cannot occur at the same time or
have no common outcomes.
e Example: Getting an odd number and getting an even number when rolling a
die

Non-mutually exclusive events, on the other hand, are events that have a common
outcome.
e Example: Getting a 3 and getting an odd number when rolling a die
