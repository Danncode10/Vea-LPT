1. Which river in the Central Luzon drains the vast fertile
valleys and plains of Nueva Ecija, Pampanga and Bulacan
exiting Manila Bay?
a. Rio Grande de Cagayan
b. Agno River
c. Pampanga River
d. Agusan River

The Pampanga River is the second largest river on the island of Luzon in the Philippines (next
to Cagayan River) and the country's fifth longest river. It is in the Central Luzon region and
traverses the provinces of Pampanga, Bulacan, and Nueva Ecija.
• Rio Grande de Cagayan in Cagayan Valley is the longest
  river in the Philippines and the largest river by discharged
  volume of water
• Agno River or Pangasinan River, is a river in the island of
  Luzon, that traverses the provinces of Benguet and
  Pangasinan
• Agusan River is the third longest river in the Philippines,
  located in the northeastern part of Mindanao island,
  draining majority of the Caraga region and some parts of
  Davao de Oro.
