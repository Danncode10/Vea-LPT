Basilio Augustín y Dávila was briefly a Spanish Governor-General of the Philippines, from
April 11 to July 24, 1898. During his tenure, the Spanish–American War began.
Ramón Blanco Erenas Riera y Polo, was a Spanish brigadier and colonial administrator.
Born in San Sebastián, he was sent to the Caribbean in 1858 and governed Cuba and
Santo Domingo. In 1861, he returned to Spain but was then sent to the Philippines.
Fermín Jáudenes y Álvarez1 was briefly a Spanish Governor-General of the Philippines
in the middle of the Philippine Revolution. During his term, two important events
occurred – the mock Battle of Manila and the surrender of the Spanish, ending over
330 years of rule.

skin. The adults are recognizable by their (usually five-point) radial
symmetry, and include starfish, sea urchins, sand dollars, and sea
cucumbers.
Hemichordata is a small phylum of marine invertebrates that
comprises the acorn worms.
