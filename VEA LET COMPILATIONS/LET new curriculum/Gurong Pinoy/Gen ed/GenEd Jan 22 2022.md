• A party-list system can help create a healthy democracy,
  providing a citizens' voice in Congress and in local
  government. The Philippine party-list system aims to
  increase the representation, particularly of "marginalized
  and underrepresented" sectors and enhance transparency
  and accountability, leading to more efficient government.
  Twenty percent of the 260 seats in the House of
  Representatives are reserved for party-list. Every 2% of
  total party-list votes cast gets a seat in the House, with
  each party allowed only a maximum of three seats.
• The party-list system is based on Republic Act 7941.
