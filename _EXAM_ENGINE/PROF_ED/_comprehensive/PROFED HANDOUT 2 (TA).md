32. Realia and computer: Which group of technologies has the highest degree of
concreteness?

Realias and computers are multisensory.

Videos, pictures, TV, films and versatile compact discs provide iconic experiences
which involve hearing and seeing only.

The types of technologies in books, imaginative literature and programmed
instructions present verbal symbols which are abstract and far from reality. For
instance, a student who is reading a material without pictures or listening to lecture
that is nothing than words will learn less. Thus, the more sensory channels possible
in interacting with a resource, the better the chance that many students can learn from
it.
