            PRESIDENTS OF THE PHILIPPINES
                   First Republic (Revolutionary Government)
       President              Term                     Important Highlights

Emilio Aguinaldo           1899–1901          First President of the Philippines;
                                              Declared Philippine Independence
                                              (June 12, 1898); introduced the
                                              Philippine Flag; Youngest President
                                              (29 years old); leader of the
                                              Revolutionary Government.


                       Commonwealth of the Philippines
       President              Term                       Important Highlights

Manuel L. Quezon           1935–1944          Father of the National Language;
                                              declared Tagalog as basis of the national
                                              language; advocated Women's Suffrage;
                                              established the National Economic
                                              Council; promoted Social Justice
                                              Program; created NLSA.

Sergio Osmeña              1944–1946          First Visayan President; rehabilitated
                                              PNB; first Speaker of the Philippine
                                              Assembly; shortest-serving elected
                                              president.


                    Second Republic (Japanese Occupation)
       President              Term                       Important Highlights

Jose P. Laurel             1943–1945          President during the
                                              Japanese-sponsored Republic; founder
                                              of Lyceum of the Philippines; served as
                                              Senator, Congressman, and Associate
                                              Justice.


                                Third Republic
       President              Term                       Important Highlights

Manuel Roxas               1946–1948          First President of the Third Republic;
                                              signed the Philippine Rehabilitation Act;
                                              focused on post-war recovery.

Elpidio Quirino            1948–1953          Established the Integrity Board; founded
                                              LASEDECO; strengthened economic
                                              recovery programs.

Ramon Magsaysay            1953–1957          Man of the Masses; established NARRA;
                                              created PCAC; suppressed the
                                              Hukbalahap Movement; signed the Rizal
                                                 Law; opened Malacañang Palace to the
                                                 public.

Carlos P. Garcia           1957–1961             Filipino First Policy; Austerity Program;
                                                 Bohlen-Serrano Agreement; known as
                                                 the Prince of Visayan Poets.

Diosdado Macapagal         1961–1965             Poor Boy from Lubao; changed
                                                 Independence Day to June 12; enacted
                                                 the Agricultural Land Reform Code;
                                                 created Land Bank and Veterans Bank.


                               Fourth Republic
       President             Term                          Important Highlights

Ferdinand E. Marcos Sr.    1965–1986             Declared Martial Law under
                                                 Proclamation No. 1081; implemented
                                                 Masagana 99; promoted infrastructure
                                                 development; removed through the EDSA
                                                 People Power Revolution (1986).


                                Fifth Republic
       President             Term                          Important Highlights

Corazon C. Aquino          1986–1992             First Female President; Mother of
                                                 Philippine Democracy; restored
                                                 democracy; promulgated the 1987
                                                 Constitution; implemented CARP.

Fidel V. Ramos             1992–1998             Resolved the Power Crisis; launched
                                                 Philippines 2000; signed peace
                                                 agreement with the MNLF; enacted IPRA
                                                 (RA 8371).

Joseph Estrada             1998–2001             Erap; Ama ng Masa; established the
                                                 National Anti-Poverty Commission;
                                                 removed through EDSA II.

Gloria Macapagal           2001–2010             Implemented EVAT; promoted Holiday
Arroyo                                           Economics; advocated Strong Republic.

Benigno Aquino III         2010–2016             P-Noy; No Wang-Wang Policy;
                                                 implemented K–12 Program; signed the
                                                 RH Law; pursued the West Philippine
                                                 Sea Arbitration Case.

Rodrigo Duterte            2016–2022             First Mindanaoan President; launched
                                                 Build, Build, Build; signed Bangsamoro
                                                 Organic Law; implemented PhilSys
                                                 National ID; signed the Universal Access
                                                 to Quality Tertiary Education Act.

Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr.   2022–Present           17th President; signed the SIM
                                                 Registration Act; approved the Maritime
                                               Zones Act; signed the New Government
                                               Procurement Act (RA 12009) and
                                               Anti-Agricultural Economic Sabotage
                                               Act.
