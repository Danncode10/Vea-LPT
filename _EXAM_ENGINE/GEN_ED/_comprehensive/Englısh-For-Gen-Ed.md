Passive vs. active voice
In an active sentence, the subject is doing the action. And it has a direct object after an action verb.

In passive voice, the target of the action gets promoted to the subject position. The doer of the action is introduced by the preposition “by”.  The subject does not do anything, nor perform any action. The verb is in the form of HV+MV.
