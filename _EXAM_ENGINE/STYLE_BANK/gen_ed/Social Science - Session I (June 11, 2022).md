5. Which of these materials is the most authentic
source of information about the life of early humans?

   B. artifacts from archaeological sites ✅
   Archaeology is the study of the human past using
material remains. These remains can be any objects that
people created, modified, or used. Portable remains are
 usually called artifacts. Artifacts include tools, clothing,
    and decorations. Non-portable remains, such as
      pyramids or post-holes, are called features.
 Archaeologists use artifacts and features to learn how
 people lived in specific times and places. They want to
 know what these people’s daily lives were like, how they
were governed, how they interacted with each other, and
             what they believed and valued.
