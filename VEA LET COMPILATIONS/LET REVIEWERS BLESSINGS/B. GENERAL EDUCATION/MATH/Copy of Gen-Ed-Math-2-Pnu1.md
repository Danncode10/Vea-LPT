a. 1/6
b. ¼
c. 1/3
d. ½
Solution:
Let S = sample space
E = event of multiples of 3
n(S) = cardinality of the sample space
n(E) = cardinality of the event
S = { 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}		E = {3,6}
P(E) = n(E)/ n(S)
= 2/6
= 1/3
