ADVANCED ORGANIZERS
•    Advance organizers are used to provide
     support for new information.
•    “Direct your attention to what is important in
     the coming material. They highlight
     relationships among ideas that will be
     presented; and remind you of relevant
     information you already have."

Exclusively for My Review Coach online LET review. Not for redistribution. © 2011 My Review Coach of MindGym Philippines.
ADVANCED ORGANIZERS
•    Advance organizers that serve to make
     appropriate prerequisite knowledge available to
     the learner by providing new information are
     called expository organizers.

•    Advance organizers that serve to build external
     connections with existing knowledge that is
     relevant to the new information by reminding
     the learner about prior knowledge are called
     comparative organizers.
