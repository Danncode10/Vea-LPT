SAMR Model
      a framework created by Dr. Ruben Puentedura
      categorizes four different degrees of classroom technology integration
(Enhancement)
 1) Substitution
    - technology acts as direct tool substitution
    - no functional change

 2) Augmentation
    - technology acts as direct tool substitution
    - w/ functional change


(Transformation)
 3) Modification
    - technology allows for significant task “redesign”

 4) Redefinition
    - technology allows for the creation of new task previously inconceivable


TPACK Model
      3 types of knowledge educators need to combine for successful educational
       technology integration.

 1) Technological Knowledge
 2) Pedagogical Knowledge
 3) Content Knowledge

Bands of Experiences
 1) Direct Purposeful
     Direct/ concrete
     Real things (Realia)
     First hand experiences (hands- on)


 2) Contrived
  a) Model - large scale or small scale representation
  b) Mock - up arrangement of detachable parts
  c) Specimens - items considered typical of a group
  d) Simulation - representation of event
  e) Games
  f) Replica - exact copy of something
 3) Dramatized
  a) Play - depicts life, character and culture
  b) Pageants - community dramas
               - small-scaled
  c) Pantomime - body movements
  d) Tableau - scene of people against a backdrop
              -make still images with their bodies to represent a scene
  e) Puppets – marionettes
  f) Role-play - unrehearsed play
 4) Demonstration
     Task is too complicated and dangerous
     Limited material
     Not enough time
 5) Study Trips
     Changes attitudes and insights
     Lasting concepts
 6) Television/ Motion Pictures
     Audio-visual
 7) Visual Symbols
  A. Drawings - concrete visual representation
  B. Cartoons - banks on symbolism
  C. Strip drawings and comics - humorous series of events
