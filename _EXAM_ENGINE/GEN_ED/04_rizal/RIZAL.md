Famous Quote of Rizal

“| have always loved my poor country, and | am
sure that | shall love her until death. If by chance
men are unjust to me, | shall still enjoy the
happy life, contented in the thought that all that |
have suffered—my past, my present, and my
future, my life, my love, pleasures—| have

Important Provisions of R.A. 1425

Section 1

Courses on the life, works, and writings of Jose
Rizal shall be included in the curricula of all
schools, colleges, and universities.

Section 2

Schools, colleges, and universities must have
adequate copies of Noli Me Tangere and E/
Filibusterismo, including other works and
biographies of Rizal.

Section 3

Translation of Rizal’s works, especially Noli Me
Tangere and E] Filibusterismo, into English,
Filipino, and other Philippine dialects shall be
encouraged.
