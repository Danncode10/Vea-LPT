20. He named our country Philippines as "Las Islas de
Felipinas" in honor to King Philip II of Spain
 a. Ferdinand Magellan
 b. Ruy Lopez de Villalobos
c. Miguel Lopez de Legaspi
d. Juan Sebastian de Elcano
It was Spanish explorer Ruy Lopez de Villalobos who in 1543 coined the name “Las
Islas Felipenas” (which later became Filipinas) in honor of Philip II, who was then
heir to the Spanish throne.
After Magellan's death, Elcano took command of the Nao Victoria as it traveled west
on its return to Spain from the Maluku Islands. The ship arrived home in September
1522, making Elcano and his crew the first people to circumnavigate the globe.

22. The officiating Priest of the First Catholic Mass
in the Philippines
a. Fr. Domingo de Salazar
  b. Fr. Manuel Rojo
  c. Fr. Pedro de Valderrama
 d. Fr. Francisco de la Cuesta
Domingo de Salazar was a Catholic prelate who served as the first Bishop of Manila which was then
newly-annexed to the Spanish Empire.
Manuel Rojo was a Mexican (originally Spanish Criollo) friar who served as the 16th Archbishop of Manila and
was Governor-General of the Philippines at the commencement of the 1762–1764 British occupation of the
Philippines.
Francisco de la Cuesta was the 12th Archbishop of Manila from 1707 to 1722, and Spanish Governor-General of
the Philippines from 1719 to 1721.
