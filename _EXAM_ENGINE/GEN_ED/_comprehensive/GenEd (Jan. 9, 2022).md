The 4 basic ethical principles are respect for autonomy,
beneficence, nonmaleficence, and justice.
1. autonomy - obliges us to respect the decisions (self
   determination) of adults who have decision-making capacity
2. beneficence - a moral obligation to act for the benefit of others
3. nonmaleficence - holds that there is an obligation not to inflict
    harm on others
4. justice - obliges us to equitably distribute benefits, risks, costs,
    and resources
 Beneficence refers to the act of helping others. Nonmaleficence is doing no harm. Beneficence is the ethical
 principle of promoting good, while nonmaleficence is to avoid causing harm. Taking a patient outside for fresh
 air is an example of beneficence, while performing multiple checks to avoid a medication error is an example of
 nonmaleficence.
