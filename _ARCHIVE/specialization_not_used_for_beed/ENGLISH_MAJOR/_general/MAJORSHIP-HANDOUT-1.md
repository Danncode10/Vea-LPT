Performatives
Explicit Performatives
Speech acts containing a performative word.
Ex. I hereby warn you to go out.


Note:
Do the “hereby” test. If the word can go with hereby, it is a performative word. 
Warn is a performative word as it goes well with the word hereby.

However,

I hereby dance.

It commonly begins with the pronoun “I” as a subject.

Dance and hereby do not go along together (awkward), hence dance is not a performative word
Implicit/ Implied Performatives
Speech acts without a performative verb. It has a tendency to be interpreted in various means.
Ex. Will you go out?

This statement could mean a request or a question to be answered by yes or no.
