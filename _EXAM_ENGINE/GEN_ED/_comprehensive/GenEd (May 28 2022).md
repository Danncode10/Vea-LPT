La Solidaridad was established to express the goal of the
Propaganda Movement towards achieving assimilation with Spain.
The first issue of La Solidaridad came out on February 15, 1889. A
fortnightly newspaper, La Solidaridad serves as the principal organ
of the Reform Movement in Spain.
La Independencia was founded by Gen. Antonio Luna and his
brother Joaquin. It was considered the most significant newspaper
of the revolution.

Gregoria de Jesus (Oriang) - known as Lakambini, wife of Andres Bonifacio,
Mother of the Philippine Revolution. She remarried another patriot, Julio Nakpil.
Melchora Aquino (Tandang Sora) risked her life feeding and sheltering
revolutionists.
Gabriela Silang was a Filipina military leader best known for her role as the female
leader of the Ilocano independence movement from Spain.
Leonor Rivera was known for being Jose Rizal’s faithful girlfriend for eleven years
(also his cousin). It is said that she was Rizal’s inspiration for the character of Maria
Clara in his novel Noli Me Tangere. She used pen names such as ‘La Cuestion del
Oriente’ and ‘Taimis’.
