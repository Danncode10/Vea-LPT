Frank Riessman (1967)
 1. Compulsive - fussy, teaches things over and over, and is
    concerned with functional order and structure, shouts in a
    loud, strong voice: “You’re going to learn”, there is no
    nonsense in the classroom.
 2. Quiet One - sincere, calm, but definite, this teacher
    commands both respect and attention.
 3. Coach - informal, earthy, and may be an athlete; he is
    physically expressive in conducting the class. Maverick
 4. Entertainer - free enough to joke and laugh with the students
 5. Secular - relaxed and informal with children; s/he will have
    lunch with them or play ball with them
 6. Academic - interested in knowledge and substance of ideas
