## RATIONALE (selected difficult items)

- **4 (B):** Apply discounts sequentially: 800 × 0.75 = 600, then 600 × 0.90 = 540. Taking 35% off at once (520) is the classic trap.
- **27 (D):** In a vacuum there is no air resistance, so all objects fall with the same acceleration (g) and land together regardless of mass.
- **28 (C):** Total of five = 5 × 24 = 120; total of remaining four = 4 × 22 = 88; removed = 120 − 88 = 32.
- **34 (C):** Botanically, any seed-bearing structure from a flower's ovary is a fruit; "vegetable" is a culinary, not scientific, label.
- **35 (A):** 2(2w + 3) = 38 → w = 8, length = 11; area = 8 × 11 = 88 cm².
- **38 (D):** Double effect permits an act with a good intended effect (relief of pain) even if a foreseen but unintended bad effect (shortened life) follows.
- **51 (C):** Rizal favored reform, not revolt; in Dapitan he taught, healed, and built — he did not lead an armed uprising. EXCEPT items reward reading the negative stem.
- **59 (A):** Girls = 24, boys = 16; after 5 boys: 21 boys of 45 = 46.7%. Forgetting to add to the total (52.5%) is the trap.
- **72 (C):** Energy rises as wavelength shortens: radio < infrared < visible < gamma.
- **73 (C):** The added 30 L raises the tank from 1/4 to 3/4, i.e. one half of capacity; capacity = 30 × 2 = 60 L.
- **75 (D):** Rizal reframed "indolence" as an effect of colonial abuse, climate, and lack of incentive — not an inborn racial trait.
- **97 (B):** Differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, …; the next gap is 12, so 30 + 12 = 42. (Also n² + n.)
- **101 (D):** "Thrifty/frugal/economical" are positive or neutral; only "stingy" is pejorative — answer keyed to connotation, not denotation.
- **111 (B):** 3(2²) − 2(2)(−3) = 12 + 12 = 24; the double negative sign is the trap.
- **128 (C):** 7.5 × 25 = 187.5 km — straightforward proportion, but the half-centimeter forces the decimal.
- **142 (A):** 2x + 3x + 4x = 9x = 180 → x = 20; largest = 4 × 20 = 80°.
- **143 (D):** The U.S. did not support the 1896 revolution; it later annexed the islands. The "LEAST accurate" stem rewards spotting the false claim.
