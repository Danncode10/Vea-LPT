Under Republic Act No. 4670 (the Magna Carta for Public School Teachers), actual
classroom teaching time is limited to a maximum of six (6) hours a day. The
remaining two hours of the standard eight-hour workday are designated for
teaching-related duties (lesson preparation, grading, paperwork).
