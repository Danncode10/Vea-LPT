                                                          PROF. ED. (Facilitating Learning)
1. It is a process that involves change in behavior resulting from experience.
A. Learning
B. Thinking
C. Teaching
D. Reading
CA. A

2. A recent study of a group of LET results reveals that those who took the LET right after year of graduation had higher chances of passing than those who postponed taking LET after year of graduation. Which law of learning explain this?
A. Frequency 
B. Exercise 
C. Continuity
D. Recency
CA. D

3. A student remembers Jose Rizal's birthday because it is one day before his birthday. How does Thorndike explain this? Bonds between stimulus and response are strengthened by:
A. Contiguity
B. Recentcy
C. Frequency
D. Contrast
CA. A

4. The use of drills in the classroom is rooted on Thorndike's law of:
A. Readiness
B. Effect
C. Exercise
D. Belongingness
CA. C

5. Sir Kier avoids drills out of context. He gives real-world Math problem for students to drill on. Sir Kier is very much convinced of which principle of learning?
A. Effective learning begins with setting clear expectations and learning outcomes
B. Learning is an active process
C. Learning is the discovery of the personal meaning of ideas
D. Learning is a cooperative and a collaborative process
CA. C

6. In his desire to finish the content of the course syllabus, Sir Janus just lectures while students listen, Which principle of learning does Sir Janus violet?
A. Learning is cooperative and collaborative process
B. Learning is the discovery if the personal meaning of ideas
C. Learning is an active process
D. Effective learning begins with setting clear expectations and learning outcomes.
CA. C

7. Teacher Vanessa believes that students need not know the intented learning outcome of her lesson.She proceeds to her learning activities at once without letting them know what they are supposed to learn for the day.Which principle of learning does Teacher Vanessa negate?
A. Learning is the discovery of the personal meaning of ideas
B. Effective learning begins with the setting clear expectations and learning outcomes.
C. Learning process is an active process.
D. Learning is a cooperative and collaborative process.
CA. B

8. Teacher Prince noticed that in group work, students just leave the work to the leader and so vowed never to give groups works again.Against which principle of learning is Teacher Prince decision?
A. Learning is the discovery of the personal meaning of ideas.
B. Learning is an Active Process.
C. Learning is a cooperative and a collaborative process.
D. Effectibe learning begins with setting clear expectations Ang learning outcomes.
CA. C

9. The teacher always checks on entry knowledge and skills before she proceeds to her new lesson. On which principle is the teacher's practice grounded?
A. Effective teaching proceeds from the concrete to the abstract.
B. Attention is essential for learning.
C. Learning increases when the lesson is relevant.
D. New learning builds on previous learning.
CA. D

10. The process of problem solving and learning are highly unique and individual. This principle means _____?
A. Students can adapt alternative problem solving models
B. Students can modify their own persoanal styles.
C. Each student becomes aware of how learning styles can be changed
D. Each student has his own distinctive style of learning and solving problems.
CA. D

11. You are required to formulate your own philosophy of education in the course teaching profession, based on Bloom's revised taxonomy, in which level of cognitive structure or processing are you?
A. Applying
B. Creating
C. Analyzing
D. Evaluating
CA. B

12. What is the foundation of all cognitive objectives that paves the way for higher thinking skills?
A. Comprehension
B. Analysis
C. Synthesis
D. Knowledge
CA. D

13. What cognitive process requires students to blend, combine, fuse, and integrate ideas or causes?
A. Application
B. Synthesis
C. Evaluation
D. Analysis
CA. B

14. It involves understanding of the external world through the use of the senses and muscles.
A. Cognitive learning
B. Affective learning
C. Psychomotor learning
D. None of the above
CA. C

15. Sir Johdel sees to it that his class sees the importance of the grammar lessons in English and so gets intrinsically motivated to learn. In which level of processing is the class expected to act?
A. Metacognitive system
B. Cognitive
C. Between cognitive and metacognitive system
D. Self-system
CA. D

16. For a teacher to serve as a facilitator of learning, according to Kendall and Marzano's new taxonomy, in which order should learners go through these processes?
I. Cognitive
II. Metacognitive
III. Self-system
A. II, I, III
B. I, III, II
C. I, II, III
D. II, III, I
CA. C

17. What question does the learner ask at the self-system level processing?
A. Why did I not learn this at first?
B. What has this lesson to do with me?
C. What is the best way to arrive at the solution?
D. How should I do this to be sure of the results?
CA. B

18. SITUATION: Teacher Carl taught the parts of a microscope, demonstrated how to focus it under the low power objective, then asked the class if the 3 students were able to focus the microscope correctly and ended his lesson citing the "don'ts" and explaining the "why's" behind the "don'ts" in focusing the microscope. Before he did all these, he asked the class if it is not important for them to learn how to focus the microscope.
Based on Kendall's and Manzano's new taxonomy, In what domains was Teacher Carl's lesson?
I. Information (Declarative knowledge)
II. Mental procedure (procedural knowledge)
III. Psychomotor procedures (Physical skills)
A. II and III
B. II only
C. I and II
D. I, II, and III
CA. D

19. Teacher Christine measures learning by outward expression of new behaviors only us such as the ability to solve math with ease. Which theory does she apply?
A. Behaviorist theory
B. Social learning theory
C. Multiple intelligence
D. Cognitivist
CA. A

20. Which is NOT TRUE of Behaviorists?
A. Learning is shaped by stimuli in the environment.
B. Learners are passive participants
C. Free will plays a role in person's development.
D. Rewards reinforce good behavior.
CA. C
