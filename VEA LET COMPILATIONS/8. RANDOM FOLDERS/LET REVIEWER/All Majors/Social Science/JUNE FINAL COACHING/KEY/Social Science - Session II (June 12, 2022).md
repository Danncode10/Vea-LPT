11. Oppressive rule of the Spanish colonizers did not go
without resistance from our ancestors. Who called
himself “King of Pangasinan” and incited people to
rebel against the unjust policies of the Spaniards?

   A. Andres Malong ✅
   Andres Malong was the leader of the short-lived but
 devastating revolt in Pangasinan in 1660-1661. A native of
  Binalatongan, Pangasinan, Malong was the province’s
    master-of-camp, the governor’s right-hand man in
dealing with the natives. He was a timawa. An Augustinian
  account described him as highly intelligent and clever.
  Although it was his job, as master-of-camp, to impress
upon his fellow Pangasinenses the advantages of having
 the Spanish overlord, he had other ideas. Unknown to his
Spanish masters, he was sowing the seeds of revolt in the
                   minds of the people.
