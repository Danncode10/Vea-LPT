Types of Management Styles
1. Autocratic - top-down approach, with one-way communication
   a. Authoritative – managers dictate
   b. Persuasive – uses persuasion skills
   c. Paternalistic – with best interest at heart
2. Democratic - managers encourage employees to give input
   a. Consultative – asks for suggestions
   b. Participative – everyone is involved
   c. Collaborative – everyone works together
   d. Transformational – growth-focused
   e. Coaching – manages like a coach
3. Laissez-faire - management takes a hands-off approach
   a. Delegative - assigns tasks
   b. Visionary – leads by inspiring the staff
