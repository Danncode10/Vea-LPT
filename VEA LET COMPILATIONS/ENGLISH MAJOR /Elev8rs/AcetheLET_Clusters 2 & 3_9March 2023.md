                     ELEMENTS OF A PLAY
1.       PLOT         The arrangement of events or incidents that the characters go through in a story
                       The plot is composed of “clearly defined problems for characters to solve.”
2.       CHARACTER    The agents of the plot. Characters provide the
                       motivations (reasons) for the events of the plot.
3.       THEME        The reason the playwright wrote the play usually as a result of
                       examining the “patterns of life”
4.       LANGUAGE      Characters facing and overcoming
                       recognizable obstacles need to express themselves
                       in “heightened language” – dramatic or narrative
5.       RHYTHM        The heart of the play. Plot, character, language, and
                       spectacle all have their individual rhythms in time.
                              The combination of all these rhythms create the impelling force of the play
                              leading to a final climax and Denouement. Rhythm creates mood.
6.       SPECTACLE   Everything that is seen or heard on stage. Actors, sets, costumes, lights and sound
lights
