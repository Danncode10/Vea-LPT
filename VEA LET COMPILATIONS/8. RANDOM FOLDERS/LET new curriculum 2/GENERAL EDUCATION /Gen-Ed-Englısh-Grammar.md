Vill. PARALLELISM
Make sentence elements parallel in thought and in form or grammatical structure.
Faulty: On weekends Carlos is either in the library OF playing tennis.

Parallel: On weekends Carlos is either browsing in the library or playing tennis.

Faulty: He enjoys reading in the evening, relaxing on the seashore, and athletics now and then.
Parallel: He enjoys reading in the evening, relaxing on the seashore, and participating in athletics
now and then.

Faulty: He spoke simply, directly, and with eloquence.
Parallel: He spoke simply, directly, and eloquently.

Faulty: The story tells of the honesty and promotion of a policeman.
Parallel: The story tells of the honesty of a policeman and of his promotion.

Faulty: Atlast he saw a fight, and voices were heard.
Parallel: At last he saw a light and heard voices.

Collective nouns such as police, committee, family, jury, audience, class, team are treated as
