1. It is the passing of culture from one generation to another.
	•	Acculturation
	•	Enculturation
	•	Cultural Relativity
	•	Value Formation

Note: Cultural Learning

ENCULTURATION: process by which individuals learn the culture of their own group, usually from birth, through observation, experience, and instruction

INDOCTRINATION: process of teaching individuals to accept a set of beliefs or ideas without encouraging critical thinking or questioning

ACCULTURATION: process of adopting certain values and practices of a culture that is not originally one's own. It's the result of contact between different cultures

ASSIMILATION: individuals fully adopt the dominant culture's traits and become socially indistinguishable from other members of that society

CULTURAL RELATIVITY: concept that a person's beliefs, values, and practices should be understood based on their own cultural context, rather than being judged by the standards of another culture

VALUES FORMATION : lifelong process by which an individual develops, internalizes, and prioritizes their core beliefs and moral principles


2. During what period were people with disabilities viewed as useless to society, and thus, must be eliminated?
	•	Era of Ridicule
	•	Era of Extermination
	•	Era of Asylum
	•	Early Beginnings of Special Education

Note: History of Special Education

ERA OF RIDICULE: people with disabilities were often the object of mockery and ridicule, with their differences viewed negatively and humorously.

ERA OF EXTERMINATION: individuals with disabilities being viewed as a burden and subjected to cruel treatments, including in some cases, being killed or abandoned.

ERA OF ASYLUM: asylums were often poorly equipped and lacked proper care, isolating individuals with disabilities from society.

EARLY BEGINNINGS OF SPECIAL EDUCATION: a shift towards and the recognition that individuals with disabilities could learn and benefit from education.
