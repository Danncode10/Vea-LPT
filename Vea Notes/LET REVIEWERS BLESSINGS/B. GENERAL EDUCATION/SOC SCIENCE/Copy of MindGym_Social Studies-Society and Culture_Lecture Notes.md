    WHAT IS DEVIANCE?
• Any behavior, belief, or condition that violates
  significant social norms in the society or group
  in which it occurs.
• Interestingly – one who is considered “deviant”
  by one category of people may be seen as
  “conformist” in another group.
• It depends on the time, place and context in
  which it occurs, and on the people who define
  and apply the norms.

GEMEINSCHAFT VS GESSELSCHAFT
• Gemeinschaft (often translated as community)
  is an association in which individuals are
  oriented to the large association.
• Individuals in Gemeinschaft are regulated by
  common mores or beliefs.
• Gemeinschafts are broadly characterized by a
  moderate division of labor, strong personal
  relationships, strong families, and relatively
  simple social institutions.

   Exclusively for My Review Coach online LET review. Not for redistribution. © 2011 My Review Coach of   Philippines.
GEMEINSCHAFT VS GESSELSCHAFT
• Gesellschaft (often translated as society).
• Gesellschaft is maintained through individuals
  acting in their own self interest.
• Emphasizes secondary relationships rather
  than familial or community ties, and there is
  generally less individual loyalty to society.
• Social cohesion in Gesellschafts typically
  derives from a more elaborate division of labor.

 MECHANICAL SOCIETIES VS
   ORGANIC SOCIETIES
• Mechanical solidarity applies to societies in
  which all members have common and shared
  social experiences, and special subdivisions
  within a society are either absent or weak.
• Because of this homogeneity, each individual is
  directly and equally attached to the society.




   Exclusively for My Review Coach online LET review. Not for redistribution. © 2011 My Review Coach of   Philippines.
  MECHANICAL SOCIETIES VS
    ORGANIC SOCIETIES
• Organic solidarity societies are formed by a
  system of different organs, each of which has a
  specific role.
• Because individuals’ tasks do not cover all
  aspects of their survival, they need to depend
  on each other to live in the society.
• They are held together to each other by one
  central organ which controls the rest of the
  organism.

 DEPENDENCY THEORY
“Dependence is a conditioning situation in which the
economies of one group of countries are conditioned
by the development and expansion of others.”

• Model of metropolis-satellite exploitation and
  extraction of economic surplus
• Has origins in the colonial eras; the satellites then
  become the colonial metropolises
• Development of the metropolis corresponds to and
  necessitates the underdevelopment of the satellites
