STUFFLEBEAM’S CIPP MODEL
	•	C - Context Evaluation: Identifies the environment, community needs, problems, and baseline goals 
	•	I - Input Evaluation: Assesses the available resources, budgets, personnel, and competing strategy designs
	•	P - Process Evaluation: Monitors the day-to-day implementation and tracks ongoing classroom execution
	•	P - Product Evaluation: Measures and interprets the final outcomes, successes, and long-term impacts
